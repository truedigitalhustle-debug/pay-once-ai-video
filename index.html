The trick

Clips are 10 seconds. So I stitch them.

Ten seconds sounds short until you treat each clip as one beat of a longer scene. Four clips became one 36-second episode. Here is the method.

Write the scene as beats, not as one video.One thing happens per clip. He calls it. It arrives. He calms it. He sends it. Each of those is its own 10-second render.
Lock your cast with reference images.Upload a sheet for every character and creature, and name them in the prompt every time they appear. That is what keeps the face the same from clip to clip.
Use a frame from clip one as the reference for clip two.Grab a still from the first clip and attach it as a reference image for the next one. The porch, the room and the creature all carry over, so the two clips look like one scene.
Change the camera angle between clips.A cut that also changes the angle looks like editing. A cut that keeps the same angle looks like a glitch.
Join them in any free editor.CapCut works. Drop the clips in order, trim the dead frames at each join, and export.
Add one layer of sound across the joins.Each clip generates its own background noise. A quiet drone under the whole thing hides where one clip ends and the next begins.

The Candy Bowl

Porch cam
10 secondsNo references neededOne static shot
SCENE CONTEXT
Porch security camera footage late on Halloween night. A bowl of candy sits on a chair by the front door, and a long pale arm reaches down from above the frame and carries the whole bowl away.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Fixed security camera mounted high in the corner of a covered front porch, looking down at 45 degrees. Center frame: a wooden chair beside the front door holding a large orange plastic bowl filled with wrapped candy. Left: the closed front door with a paper bat decoration. Right: porch railing wrapped in fake cobwebs, two porch steps, a carved pumpkin with a lit candle on the top step. Background: a dark lawn with fallen leaves.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous static shot, the camera stays fixed and does not cut.

OPTICS
84 degree wide security lens, deep focus.

CAMERA
Locked-off porch security camera, low-light color video with soft sensor noise and mild compression artifacts.

ACTION
0s to 3s, the porch is empty and still, the pumpkin candle flickers, a cobweb strand sways. 3s to 6s, a long thin pale arm, three times the length of a human arm, lowers slowly into frame from above the top edge, the elbow bending the wrong way, long fingers spreading. 6s to 8s, the hand closes around the rim of the candy bowl with all five fingers and lifts the whole bowl straight up, a few wrapped candies spilling onto the chair seat. 8s to 10s, the arm and bowl rise out of the top of the frame, the chair is left empty with three candies on it, and the pumpkin candle goes out.

PHYSICS
The bowl has weight and tilts as it lifts, candies tumble and bounce on the wooden seat, the arm moves slowly and smoothly at 1 km/h, the cobwebs sway.

LIGHTING
One warm porch lamp out of frame at upper left, 2700K, soft orange glow from the pumpkin candle, deep shadow beyond the steps, the pale arm catching the lamp light.

AUDIO
Quiet night air, a faint wooden creak from the porch roof above, candy wrappers rustling, three small taps as candies drop on the chair, a soft puff as the candle dies. All sound is ambient with no speech and no music.

STYLE
Photoreal found-footage security camera recording, natural grain.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The camera stays fixed. Only the arm enters the frame, always from the top edge. The hand has five long fingers with natural joints. The porch stays empty of people. Clean image with no on-screen text.

The Jack-o'-Lanterns

Doorbell cam
10 secondsNo references neededOne static shot
SCENE CONTEXT
Doorbell camera footage at 3 AM on a suburban front porch decorated for Halloween. Five carved jack-o'-lanterns line the walkway, and their candles go out one at a time toward the camera until the nearest pumpkin turns to face the lens.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Fixed doorbell camera view from chest height beside the front door. Foreground: two porch steps with one large carved jack-o'-lantern on the top step, 1 meter from the lens, candle glowing inside. Midground: a straight concrete walkway 10 meters long with four more lit jack-o'-lanterns spaced along its left edge. Background: a dark empty street with one streetlight, fallen leaves on the lawn.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous static shot, the camera stays fixed and does not cut.

OPTICS
107 degree ultra-wide doorbell lens, slight barrel distortion, deep focus.

CAMERA
Locked-off doorbell camera, low-light color video with soft sensor noise and mild compression artifacts.

ACTION
0s to 2s, all five candles flicker steadily, leaves drift across the walkway. 2s to 6s, the candle in the farthest pumpkin goes dark, then one second later the next pumpkin goes dark, then the next, then the next, each one closer to the camera, a thin line of smoke rising from each. 6s to 8s, only the large pumpkin on the top step is still lit, its candle flame leaning sideways as if blown. 8s to 10s, the large pumpkin slowly rotates in place by itself until its glowing carved face points straight into the lens, then its candle goes out and the porch is dark.

PHYSICS
Candle flames flicker and lean in the breeze, smoke curls upward, dry leaves skitter along the concrete, the pumpkin turns with a heavy scraping drag.

LIGHTING
Warm orange candle glow from inside each pumpkin as the main light, 2200K, a weak cold streetlight in the distance, the scene getting darker as each candle dies.

AUDIO
Night wind, dry leaves scraping concrete, a soft puff as each candle goes out, a slow heavy scraping sound as the pumpkin turns, then silence. All sound is ambient with no speech and no music.

STYLE
Photoreal found-footage doorbell camera recording, natural grain.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The camera stays fixed. The porch and walkway stay empty of people. Five pumpkins only, each staying in its place. Clean image with no on-screen text.
Why these work: the camera never moves, only one strange thing happens, and every second is written out. The more exact the prompt, the less the model has to guess.
